# Acme Bank help centre

The Basic Savings account requires a minimum balance of 1000 rupees. The Basic Savings account includes a RuPay debit card. The Basic Savings account charges no monthly fee.

The Premium Savings account requires a minimum balance of 25000 rupees. The Premium Savings account includes a Platinum debit card, free cheque books and priority banking. The Premium Savings account is available in Mumbai and Delhi. The Basic Savings account is available in every city.

The RuPay debit card has a daily ATM limit of 20000 rupees. The Platinum debit card has a daily ATM limit of 100000 rupees. The Platinum debit card includes airport lounge access.

## Loans

The Home Loan requires a credit score of 700. The Home Loan has an interest rate of 8.5 percent. The Home Loan is eligible for tax benefits. The Personal Loan requires a credit score of 750. The Personal Loan charges a processing fee of 2 percent. The Personal Loan has an interest rate of 11 percent.

## Support and security

Priority banking provides a dedicated relationship manager. Phone banking provides help within 5 minutes. Customers on any account can use phone banking.

Customers can block a lost card using the mobile app. The mobile app supports card blocking and fund transfers. The zero liability policy covers unauthorized transactions reported within 3 days. The zero liability policy refunds the full amount.

The early closure fee applies to the Personal Loan only. The early closure fee costs 4 percent of the outstanding balance.
