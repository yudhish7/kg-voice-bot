# Acme Broadband help centre

The Fiber 300 plan includes a free router and a static IP address. The Fiber 300 plan costs 49 dollars per month. The Fiber 300 plan requires a 12 month contract.

The Fiber 1000 plan includes the Mesh Pro router, a static IP address and priority support. The Fiber 1000 plan costs 79 dollars per month.

The Mesh Pro router supports WiFi 6. The Mesh Pro router supports up to 120 devices. The free router supports WiFi 5.

The Fiber 300 plan is available in Springfield and Shelbyville. The Fiber 1000 plan is available in Springfield.

## Support and refunds

Priority support provides a callback within 15 minutes. Standard support provides a callback within 24 hours. Customers on any plan can contact standard support.

The 30 day guarantee covers every new plan. The 30 day guarantee refunds the full monthly fee. A customer cancelling after 30 days pays an early termination fee. The early termination fee applies to the Fiber 300 plan only.

If your internet is down, first restart the router and wait two minutes. The outage credit applies after 4 hours without service. The outage credit equals one day of service for every full day of outage.
